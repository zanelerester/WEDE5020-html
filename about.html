About The Chocolate Den

South Africa's first shop dedicated to helping everyday people mould, fill and package their own chocolates.

Our story

Where It All Began

The Chocolate Den was established in the early 1980s by Felicity Phillips. It was the first shop of its kind in South Africa, offering chocolate moulds, moulding chocolate, fillings and packaging to everyone and anyone who wanted to mould and create delicious chocolates.

What started as a small counter of moulds and chocolate buttons has grown into a one-stop shop for chocolatiers, home bakers, cake decorators and small food businesses. We still believe what Felicity believed: you don't need to be a professional to make chocolates that look and taste amazing.

Today we stock hundreds of products, from easy-melt moulding chocolate and flavouring oils to cake boxes and clear gift packaging, and our team is always happy to share tips and advice.
