High-quality greaseproof paper liners for baking muffins and cupcakes. They fit standard muffin pans, keep your pans clean and make it easy to lift out baked goods without sticking.

  • Pack size: Pack of 20
  • Delivery: 2-5 working days nationwide
  • Bulk orders: request a quote for 10% off 10+ units

Our paper muffin cup liners are a kitchen staple for anyone who bakes. The greaseproof paper holds its shape in the oven, keeps colours bright and peels away cleanly from the cake. Use them for muffins, cupcakes, mini cheesecakes or even for serving homemade chocolates and sweets.

MaterialGreaseproof paper
SizeStandard (50 mm base)
ColourWhite
Oven safeUp to 220 °C
  • Fill liners two-thirds full for a nicely domed muffin.
  • Place liners in a muffin pan so they keep their shape while baking.
  • Let cupcakes cool completely before icing.
