Our story

A Family Business Since the Early 1980s

The Chocolate Den was founded by Felicity Phillips with one simple idea: anyone should be able to make beautiful chocolates at home. Four decades later we still stock everything from moulding chocolate and fillings to packaging, so hobby bakers and small businesses can create, gift and sell their own treats.

  • Friendly advice from experienced chocolatiers
  • Products for beginners and professionals
  • Bulk pricing for small businesses
